Cookie Policy

1. Our use of cookies

This website uses Google Analytics cookies to count visits and see which pages people use, so we can improve the site. We only set these cookies if you click "Accept" on the cookie banner. If you click "Reject", or ignore the banner, no analytics cookies are set.

2. Cookies we use

Name Set by Purpose Expires
_ga Google Analytics Tells visits apart so we can count unique visitors 2 years
_ga_<ID> Google Analytics Keeps track of the current visit 2 years

We also save your cookie choice in your browser's local storage, under the name logan-cookie-consent, so the banner doesn't keep appearing. This is needed to remember your choice, so it doesn't require consent.

3. Google Analytics

Google Analytics tells us things like how many people visited, which pages they viewed, roughly where in the world they are, and what device they used. It doesn't tell us who you are. We've turned off Google's advertising features, so this data isn't used to show you ads. Google processes the data in the United States. Read how Google uses this data.

4. Changing your choice

You can change your mind at any time. Open , which is also linked in the footer of every page. If you switch to "Reject", we'll delete the analytics cookies from your browser.

5. Other services

To display the site, your browser also loads files from Tailwind CSS (cdn.tailwindcss.com) and Phosphor Icons (unpkg.com), and sends the booking form through Formspree (formspree.io). These services receive your IP address to deliver the files, but they don't set cookies through this site.

6. More information

You can also delete cookies in your browser settings. For more about cookies, see the ICO's guide. Questions can go to bookings@example.com. See our privacy policy for how we handle personal information.
