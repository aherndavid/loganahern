Cookie Policy

1. Our use of cookies

This website does not set any cookies, and it doesn't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. That's why you won't see a cookie banner.

2. Third-party services

To display the site, your browser loads a few files from other services:

  • Tailwind CSS (cdn.tailwindcss.com) for page styling
  • Phosphor Icons (unpkg.com) for icons
  • Formspree (formspree.io), only when you send the booking form

Like any website, these services receive your IP address so they can deliver files to your browser. They do not set cookies through this site.

3. If this changes

If we ever add analytics or other non-essential cookies, we'll ask for your permission before any are set, and we'll update this page.

4. Managing cookies

You can view and delete cookies at any time in your browser settings. For more about cookies, see the ICO's guide. Questions about this policy can go to bookings@example.com. See our privacy policy for how we handle personal information.
